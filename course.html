Original concise teaching outline

Course 1 · Foundations of Computational Chemistry

From the motivation and method landscape to quantum mechanics, Hartree–Fock, electron correlation, basis sets, molecular dynamics, and potential energy surfaces.

  1. Introduction: What Is Computational Chemistry and Why Does It Matter?
  2. The Schrödinger Equation: History, Meaning, and Wavepackets
  3. The Hydrogen Atom and Atomic Orbitals
  4. Many-Electron Systems: Hartree–Fock and Self-Consistent Field Theory
  5. Electron Correlation: MP2, Configuration Interaction, and Coupled Cluster
  6. Basis Sets and the Basis-Set Limit
  7. Force Fields, Molecular Dynamics, and Monte Carlo Methods
  8. Potential Energy Surfaces, Geometry Optimization, Vibrations, and Thermochemistry

Course 2 · Density Functional Theory with ORCA and Gaussian

Learn the foundations of DFT and apply electronic-structure methods using widely used quantum-chemistry software.

  1. Hohenberg–Kohn Theorems and Kohn–Sham DFT
  2. Exchange–Correlation Functionals, Basis Sets, and Dispersion
  3. Practical ORCA: Input Preparation, Job Execution, and Output Files
  4. Practical Gaussian: Route Sections, Checkpoints, and Cube Files
  5. Reading Outputs, Energy Calculations, and Troubleshooting
  6. Time-Dependent DFT and Electronic Absorption Spectra
